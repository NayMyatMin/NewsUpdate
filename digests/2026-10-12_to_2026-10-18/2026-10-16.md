**Source:** ArXiv cs.AI | 2026-10-16 04:00 UTC | **Relevance:** 9.0/10

> INFUSE introduces a comprehensive end-to-end benchmark and taxonomy for indirect prompt injection (IPI) across long-horizon agent workflows, with 40 tasks and 56 attack scenarios; the authors evaluate 19 LLM families across 41,824 adversarial trajectories and show mean attack success ~19.6% (LLM-eval) and substantial variability (3.2–41.9%). **Why it matters:** Provides the first large-scale, realistic evaluation surface for IPI in autonomous workflows, giving an essential tool to measure and compare defenses for agentic systems used in Huawei deployments.

**Why it matters:** Highlights critical weaknesses in common TEE-based IP protection for on-device LLMs and proposes a practical countermeasure, directly relevant to protecting Huawei’s on-device model IP and secure inference strategy.
